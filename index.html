Botanical Essentials

Simple Rules for Thriving Plants

Caring for indoor greens is a relaxing ritual. Follow three core principles to keep your foliage lush and planters pristine.

Mindful Watering

Check topsoil dryness before pouring. Most plants prefer deep, infrequent soaks with thorough drainage over daily sprinkles.

Pro tip: Empty outer pots after 15 mins

Filtered Natural Light

Position near east or north windows. Intense direct sun can scorch delicate leaves, while sheer curtains create ideal diffusion.

Pro tip: Rotate pots weekly for even growth

Humidity & Climate

Keep room temperature between 18–24°C. Group plants together or place them on pebble trays to create natural micro-humidity.

Pro tip: Keep away from hot radiators
